Frequently Asked Questions About Autism

Question: Is autism a disease that can be cured?
Answer: No, autism is a neurodevelopmental condition, which means the brain develops and processes information differently. It is a lifelong variation in human development, not a medical disease to be cured. Therapeutic interventions are designed to build communication and coping skills, not to remove autism.

Question: At what age can an accurate autism diagnosis be made?
Answer: A reliable and accurate diagnosis of autism spectrum disorder can often be made by an experienced professional by 18 to 24 months of age. Early developmental screening tools like the M-CHAT are used by pediatricians during early childhood checkups.

Question: What is masking in autism?
Answer: Autism masking occurs when an autistic individual consciously or unconsciously suppresses their natural autistic traits (such as stimming or intense interests) and mimics neurotypical social behaviors to fit into social environments or avoid bullying. Masking takes immense cognitive effort and can lead to severe mental exhaustion and burnout.
